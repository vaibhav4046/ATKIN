## 7. Grammar Selection & Justification
- **Chosen Grammar**: **Chaptered Editorial with Embedded Live Evidentiary Surface**.
- **Why Other Grammars Lost**:
  - *Filmic One-Shot*: Lost. Proofline is a legal workbench with multi-stage inspection tools. A continuous camera flight trivializes evidentiary rigor.
  - *Continuous World*: Lost. Legal litigation requires distinct documentary chapters (Disclosures, Pleadings, Exhibits), not a continuous landscape.
  - *Typographic Poster*: Lost. Does not provide the dense tabular inspection surfaces needed for court exhibits.
  - *Gallery / Catalog*: Lost. Designed for product variations and menus, not forensic dispute resolution.
  - *Rhythmic Cutlist*: Lost. Too fast; legal proof requires deliberate reading and examination.
